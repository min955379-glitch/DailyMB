# Store listing (draft)

Name: DailyMB
Subtitle: Daily quiz answers and streak tracker

Description:
DailyMB collects the day's 5 quiz answers each morning from several public
websites and cross-checks them. An answer is only marked verified when at
least two independent sites agree. View answers as a short letter list or
with the full answer text, copy them in one tap, track your 5-day reward
streak (25, 50, 80, 100, 125 MB), and get a morning notification plus an
evening reminder if you have not claimed yet. Works offline with the last
cached answers, in English or Urdu, with full dark mode.

DailyMB is built for the daily "Test Your Skills" quiz in the My Telenor
app (plain-text reference only). Independent helper app, not affiliated
with or endorsed by any mobile operator. Answers come from public websites
and may occasionally be wrong; always check.

Keywords: daily quiz, answers today, streak tracker, free MB, English quiz
