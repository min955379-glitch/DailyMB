# DailyMB Privacy Policy (short form, v1.0)

DailyMB needs no account and collects no personal data.
- Stored on the server: only your device's Expo push token, platform,
  notification time preference and timezone, so the morning push can be sent.
  Push tokens are not readable with the public app key and are never sold or shared.
- Stored on your phone only: your settings, claimed-day streak marks, and a
  cached copy of recent answers (so the app works offline).
- The optional Android Auto-Assist (sideload build only) keeps its step log
  on the phone only and does not read or store personal data.
- Delete your push token from the server by uninstalling the app, or contact
  the developer to have it removed.

Disclaimer: Independent helper app, not affiliated with or endorsed by any
mobile operator. Answers come from public websites and may occasionally be
wrong; always check.
